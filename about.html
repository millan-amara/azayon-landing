About Azayon

Built here.
Built for here.

Azayon is a CRM built specifically for African SMBs — not an afterthought port of a Western product, but something designed from the ground up for how businesses on this continent actually operate.

Why we built this

Every CRM tool we tried was built for companies in San Francisco or London. The pricing was in dollars, the features were designed for 50-person sales teams, and the onboarding assumed you had a dedicated IT department.

Meanwhile, a hardware shop in Nairobi, a logistics company in Lagos, an agency in Accra — they all had the same problem: leads falling through the cracks, no clear picture of the pipeline, follow-ups forgotten.

Azayon is the answer to that. Simple enough to set up in an afternoon. Powerful enough to run a serious sales operation. Priced so it makes sense for your business.

What we believe

Good software should get out of your way. The best CRM is the one your team actually uses — not the one with the most features, but the one that fits how you work.

African businesses deserve first-class tools built for their context — local pricing, local payment methods, and an understanding of how business gets done here.

Built for simplicity

Every feature earns its place. If it doesn't make your sales process easier, it doesn't exist.

Africa first

Local-currency pricing, mobile money, and features designed for the realities of doing business across the continent.

Automation as default

Manual follow-ups shouldn't exist. We automate the routine so you focus on the relationships.

Honest pricing

No per-seat traps. No dollar conversion. One flat rate that grows with your business, not against it.

The team

Small team.
Big ambition.

Azayon is built by Peskaya Limited, a Kenyan technology company founded in 2025. We're a small team obsessed with building tools that actually work for African businesses — and we plan to roll out across the continent, one country at a time.
