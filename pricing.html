Common questions

What happens after my 7-day trial?

Your data stays safe. You'll be prompted to subscribe to continue using Growth features. If you don't subscribe, you move to the Free plan automatically — nothing is deleted.

How does M-Pesa payment work?

At checkout you'll be redirected to Paystack where you can pay via M-Pesa, debit card, or bank transfer. Payments are processed securely and your subscription activates instantly.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel any time from your settings page. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period. No questions asked.
